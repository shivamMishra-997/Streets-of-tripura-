About Us

/ 02

Streets of Tripura began as five photographers meeting every Sunday at Battala market, cameras loaded, no plan except to walk. Today we are a community of frame-makers documenting the everyday life of Agartala, Udaipur, Sabroom and the hills beyond.

We believe the street doesn't need staging — it needs attention. Our members shoot markets, monsoons, tea stalls, tribal fairs and quiet corners, building an honest visual archive of a state often left out of the frame.

Every month we run open street-walks, portfolio critiques, and a print exchange — free for anyone who shows up with a camera and curiosity.
